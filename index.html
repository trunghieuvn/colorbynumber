FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Most pictures, hints and the daily picture are free. Premium unlocks the biggest masterpieces and unlimited hints.

Does it need internet?

No. Pictures ship inside the app. Internet is only used for purchases and anonymous crash/usage statistics.

Where do the pictures come from?

Public-domain paintings from Wikimedia Commons and CC0 illustrations. Credits are listed in the app under More › Artwork credits.

iPhone?

Android first. An iOS version is planned.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open More › Restore purchases while signed in to the Google account that bought it.
