Bit Pals is a retro color by number game for anyone who grew up with chunky pixels and bright arcade screens. Every picture is a tiny 8-bit world: little monsters, robots, rockets, snacks, power-ups and pixel pals waiting for their colors. FAQ

Is Bit Pals free?

Yes. You can color free pictures, the daily picture and use free hints without paying. The free version shows ads. Premium removes ads, unlocks the premium pictures and gives unlimited hints.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Pictures already on your phone can be colored anywhere. The internet is used for ads, purchases, anonymous statistics and new content.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your phone.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the account that bought it, open Bit Pals, then More › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Bit Pals › Cancel. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

Where do the pictures come from?

The pixel pictures are made for the app. Credits for anything adapted from a free-to-use source are in the app under More › Artwork credits.
