FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Most sprites, the daily puzzle and free hints cost nothing. Premium unlocks the largest pages and unlimited hints.

Does it need internet?

No. Every picture ships inside the app. The network is only used for purchases, anonymous statistics and the optional rewarded video.

Where does the art come from?

The pixel sprites are drawn for Pixelo. Credits for anything adapted are listed in the app under More › Artwork credits.

What is Play together?

Two phones on the same Wi-Fi can colour one picture at once, or race to finish the same sprite. Free players host one room a day.

How do I restore a purchase?

More › Restore purchases, signed in to the Google account that bought it.
