FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Most pages, the daily picture and free hints cost nothing. Premium unlocks the biggest pictures and unlimited hints.

Does it need internet?

No. Every picture ships inside the app. The network is only used for purchases, anonymous statistics and the optional rewarded video.

Where do the pictures come from?

Public-domain paintings from Wikimedia Commons, CC0 illustrations and art drawn for Colorio. Credits are in the app under More › Artwork credits.

What is Play together?

Two phones on the same Wi-Fi can colour one picture at once, or race to finish the same page. Free players host one room a day.

How do I restore a purchase?

More › Restore purchases, signed in to the Google account that bought it.
