Common questions

My Premium disappeared after changing phone

Sign in to the same Google account, open the app, then More › Restore purchases.

Save to gallery does nothing

On Android 9 and lower the app needs the Storage permission - allow it when asked, or enable it in Settings › Apps › Calmly › Permissions. Saved images are in Pictures › Color by Number.

A region is too small to tap

Pinch to zoom, or tap near it - the app picks the closest region of the selected colour. A hint fills the largest remaining region for you.

Can I reset a picture?

Long-press support is planned. For now, finished pictures can be replayed from My Works.

Request a picture or report an artwork problem

Email us with the picture name. All artwork is public-domain or CC0; credits are in More › Artwork credits.
